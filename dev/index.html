grid-doctor

Your lat/lon data goes to rehab and comes out HEALed.

Converts climate and Earth-system data from regular, curvilinear and unstructured grids, and from satellite swaths, to multi-resolution HEALPix pyramids in Zarr, on local disk or S3.

What it does¶

  • Automatic grid detection

    Regular (ERA5, CMIP), curvilinear and rotated-pole (NEMO, CORDEX, WRF) and unstructured (ICON) grids are recognised from the dataset itself.

  • Conservative remapping

    Conservative and nearest-neighbour remapping with ESMF weights, on the sphere or the WGS84 ellipsoid.

  • A shared weights store

    Weights are computed once per source grid, HEALPix level and method, and reused by everyone pointing at the same directory.

  • Point binning

    Swaths, stations and trajectories, where every sample has its own position, are binned into HEALPix cells.

  • Pyramids

    From the finest level down to level 0, with mean or mode coarsening and valid fractions for masked fields.

  • Parallel writes

    Zarr stores in one call, or split into independent tasks for HPC array jobs.

  • Region selection

    By bounding box, cone or cell list, also for stores without coordinate arrays.

  • CF metadata

    The CF healpix grid mapping and a record of how every level was built.

Regridding and coarsening run lazily on dask arrays.

Quick example¶

import xarray as xr

import grid_doctor as gd

ds = xr.open_mfdataset("data/*.nc")
pyramid = gd.create_healpix_pyramid(ds)  # finest level from the grid resolution
gd.save_pyramid(pyramid, "/path/to/output", mode="w")

create_healpix_pyramid computes the remapping weights on the first run and takes them from the weights store afterwards.
